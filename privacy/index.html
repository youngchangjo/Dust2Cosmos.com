Privacy

Dust to Cosmos: Universe Scale is designed with your privacy in mind. The iOS and iPadOS app does not collect, store, or share your personal data. All app processing happens entirely on your device.

This website may use Vercel Web Analytics and Google Analytics to understand aggregate page traffic, referrals, device categories, and browser information. Website analytics are separate from the Dust to Cosmos app and are used only to improve the public website.

If you contact us by email for support, we will use the information you provide only to respond to your inquiry and resolve your issue. We do not use your email for marketing without explicit consent.

We are committed to maintaining a minimal data footprint to ensure your creative work remains yours alone.
